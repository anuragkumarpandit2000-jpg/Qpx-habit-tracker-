# QPX — Quantum Progress Experience

## Product Vision

Build a world-class mobile application called **QPX (Quantum Progress Experience)**.

QPX is not a habit tracker.

QPX is a Real-Life RPG Progression System where users transform themselves through discipline, learning, fitness, creation, consistency, and self-development.

The app should make users feel like they are playing a premium RPG game while upgrading their real life.

The experience should combine:

* RPG Progression Game
* Habit Tracking System
* Productivity Dashboard
* Self-Improvement Platform
* Character Evolution Simulator
* Achievement & Reward System

The entire application should feel immersive, futuristic, premium, emotional, motivating, and highly engaging.

---

# Core Philosophy

Users do not chase results.

Users chase:

* XP
* Levels
* Ranks
* Streaks
* Achievements
* Character Evolution
* Rewards

Real-world success becomes the outcome of consistent progression.

Primary Philosophy:

Progress > Perfection

Missing a task should have consequences, but should never completely destroy long-term progress.

---

# First-Time Onboarding System

Immediately after account creation or first login, present a premium onboarding questionnaire.

The purpose is to create a personalized player profile.

The app must ask 10 questions.

### Questions

1. What is your age?
2. What is your current height?
3. What is your current weight?
4. What is your primary goal?
5. What are your top three priorities?
6. What skills do you want to learn?
7. How many hours can you dedicate daily?
8. What is your biggest weakness right now?
9. What habits do you want to build?
10. What type of person do you want to become in the next 1 year?

Using these answers, automatically generate:

* Player Profile
* Starting Quests
* Recommended Goals
* Personalized Progress Plan
* Initial Character Attributes
